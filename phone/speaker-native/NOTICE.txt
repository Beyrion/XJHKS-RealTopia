RealTopia consecutive speaker identification.

Android library: Alibaba MNN 3.6, bundled sherpa-mnn 1.10.46,
https://github.com/alibaba/MNN, Apache-2.0. Kotlin speaker JNI wrappers
adapted from k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx v1.13.8 (Apache-2.0) to sherpa-mnn.
All model inference uses MNN; ONNX Runtime is not linked or packaged.
CAM++ Chinese speaker embeddings: Alibaba 3D-Speaker / iic,
speech_campplus_sv_zh-cn_16k-common, Apache-2.0.
Pyannote segmentation-3.0 FP32 source: k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx,
original Copyright (c) 2022 CNRS, MIT.
Both models exported with MNNConvert 3.6, retaining original metadata.
Model interchange files are build inputs in the host cache only.
Corresponding license texts accompany these assets.

This is turn segmentation and temporary voice clustering, not a source
separator. It does not produce isolated overlapping-speaker audio tracks.
No performance or identity accuracy is guaranteed for a user's room/noise.
